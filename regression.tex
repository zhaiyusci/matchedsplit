\section*{Matchedsplit 0.5 regression examples}
Nested pairs across three rows:
\[
\begin{matchedsplit}
F &= \left( a+\left[\frac{x}{y}+b \\
  &\quad +c\right]+d \\
  &\quad +\frac{p}{q}\right)
\end{matchedsplit}
\]
Three levels, grouped arguments, subscripts and explicit row spacing:
\[
\begin{matchedsplit}
G &= \left\{ \frac{\frac{a}{b}}{\frac{c}{d}} + \left[ x_i^2+\left( u \\*[4pt]
  &\quad +v\right)+w\right] \\[2pt]
  &\quad +z\right\}
\end{matchedsplit}
\]
Middle delimiters and invisible delimiters:
\[
\begin{matchedsplit}
S &= \left\{ x\in\mathbb{R} \middle| \frac{x}{2}>0 \\
  &\quad \middle| x<10 \right\} + \left.\frac{f(x)}{g(x)} \\
  &\quad +h(x)\right|_{x=0}
\end{matchedsplit}
\]
Independent pairs (the first pair should remain small):
\[
\begin{matchedsplit}
H &= \left(a+b\right)+\left[\frac{\frac{x}{y}}{\frac{u}{v}} \\
  &\quad +1\right]
\end{matchedsplit}
\]
